## The short version

Most of what you enter stays only on your device. If you choose to sign in
with a Google account or a phone number, your data is also backed up to a
private cloud database tied to that account, so it can follow you to a new
phone. If you use Contact us, that one message is emailed to the developer.
Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond what's
described below.

## What the app stores

On your device always:

- Name, date of birth, gender, country, and contact details you enter
- Medications, doses, schedules and which doses you confirmed
- Vital sign readings, symptoms and side effects you record — including
  readings synced in automatically from Health Connect or a paired
  Bluetooth device, if you turn those on
- Water, meals, steps and exercise you log
- Smoking status and supplements, if you enter them
- Emergency contacts you add
- A profile photo, if you add one or sign in with Google
- Your most recent GPS coordinates, if you allow location

## Cloud backup, if you sign in

If you sign in with a **Google account** or a **phone number**, the data
listed above is also backed up to a private cloud database (Google
Firebase/Firestore), tied to that Google account or phone number. This is
what lets your data follow you if you reinstall the app or move to a new
phone by signing in again the same way.

If you instead choose **local-only** setup (no Google or phone sign-in),
none of your data ever leaves the device — there is nothing to back up
anywhere, and losing the device means losing the data unless you've used
**Export my data** in Profile.

Only you can access this cloud backup, through your own sign-in. The
developer does not browse or read individual users' health data; the cloud
database exists to sync your own data back to your own devices, not to
share it with anyone else, including us.

## Signing in

- **Google sign-in** shares your name, email address and profile photo with
  the app, via Google's own sign-in process.
- **Phone sign-in** verifies your phone number by text message, via
  Firebase Authentication (part of Google Firebase).

Neither is required — you can use the app fully with a **local-only**
profile instead, with no account and no data leaving the device at all.

## Health Connect and paired devices

If you turn on Health Connect or connect a Bluetooth device (like a heart
rate monitor), the app reads readings those already have — steps, heart
rate, blood pressure, and similar — the same way any app you've granted
that permission to would. Those readings are then stored and treated
exactly like anything you entered by hand: on the device, and in your cloud
backup if you're signed in.

## Contact us

If you use the Contact us form in Profile, your name, email address, and
message are sent to the developer to reply to — currently via Cloudflare
(hosting the form's endpoint) and Resend (sending the email). No other data
from the app is included automatically.

## What we do NOT do

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no ad tracking, and no crash
reporting that leaves your device beyond what's described above. We do not
sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers, under any
circumstances.

## Permissions and why

- **Notifications** — to show medication reminders at the times you set
- **Location** — to display your coordinates on the emergency screen so you
  can read them to a dispatcher. Stored on the device; not sent anywhere by
  the app itself.
- **Motion sensor** — to count steps, read entirely on the device
- **Bluetooth** — only if you connect a supported device, to read its
  readings
- **Health Connect** — only if you turn it on, to read readings already
  collected by apps you've separately authorized

You can refuse any of these. The rest of the app continues to work.

## Sharing

Beyond the cloud backup (accessible only to you, via your own sign-in) and
the Contact us form (which you initiate), we do not sell, share or transfer
your data. When you generate a report or export your data, you choose where
it goes — printing it, saving it, or sending it to your doctor is your
action, not ours.

## Deleting your data

Delete a profile in the app and its data is removed from the device and,
if you were signed in, from the cloud backup too. Uninstalling the app
removes everything stored locally. If you'd like your cloud backup deleted
and have trouble doing this from within the app, contact us using the email
below.

## Backups

If you use a **local-only** profile, your data is not backed up anywhere
by us — losing or resetting your phone, or clearing the app's storage,
loses it permanently unless you've used **Export my data** in Profile
first. Signing in with Google or a phone number backs your data up
automatically, as described above.

## Not a medical device

Health Chart is a personal record keeping tool. It shows published
reference ranges for general information. It does not diagnose, treat or
prescribe, and it is not a substitute for your doctor, nurse or pharmacist.
Never change or stop a prescribed medicine based on anything this app
shows you. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

## Children

This app is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.
