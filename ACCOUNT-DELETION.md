# Delete your account and data — Health Chart

## Delete specific data, without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove individual
entries. Inside Health Chart, every item you've logged has its own delete
option, reachable directly from the screen it's listed on:

- **Medications** — Medications tab → open the medication → **Remove**
- **Vital readings** — Vitals tab → tap the reading → **Delete**
- **Symptoms and side effects** — Medications tab → Symptoms/Side effects
  section → **Remove**
- **Emergency contacts** — Profile → Emergency contacts → **Remove**
- **Diagnoses** — Profile → Diagnoses → **Remove**
- **Supplements** — Medications tab → Supplements section → **Remove**
- **Workouts / exercise entries** — Sport tab → open the entry → **Remove**

Each of these deletes immediately — from the device and, if you're
signed in, from the cloud backup too. There's no waiting period and
nothing is retained after you delete an individual entry this way. The
rest of your account and data is unaffected.

If you no longer have the app installed and want specific data types
deleted rather than the whole account, email **[[ahmadjb.chelsea@gmail.com]
]**
with the subject line "Delete specific data," naming which data type(s)
from the list above you'd like removed and the email address on your
account. We will complete this within 30 days. Data types you don't name
are kept as normal.

## Delete your whole account

### In the app (fastest)

Open Health Chart → **Profile** → **Log out** (top of the screen) → on
the sign-in screen, find your account under "Continue as" → tap the **×**
next to it → **Delete permanently**. This removes your data from the
device immediately and, if you were signed in, from the cloud backup too.

### Without the app installed

If you no longer have the app installed, email
**[[ahmadjb.chelsea@gmail.com]
]** from the email address associated with your
account (or include your account's email in the message), with the
subject line "Delete my account."

We will delete your account and all associated data from our systems
within 30 days of receiving your request.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile (name, date of birth, gender, country, contact details)
- All medications, doses, and dose history
- All vital sign readings, symptoms, and side effects
- Water, meal, exercise, and weight logs
- Emergency contacts
- Your profile photo, if you added one
- Your account's sign-in record with us

Nothing is retained after deletion, with one exception: if you ever used
**Contact us** inside the app, that specific message (sent to us by email
at the time) follows our normal email retention, separately from your
in-app account — deleting your account does not retroactively delete a
message you already sent us.
