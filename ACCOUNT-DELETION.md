# Delete your account and data — Health Chart

## In the app (fastest)

Open Health Chart → **Profile** → **Log out** (top of the screen) → on
the sign-in screen, find your account under "Continue as" → tap the **×**
next to it → **Delete permanently**. This removes your data from the
device immediately and, if you were signed in, from the cloud backup too.

## Without the app installed

If you no longer have the app installed, email
**[ahmadjb.chelsea@gmail.com]** from the email address associated with your
account (or include your account's email in the message), with the
subject line "Delete my account."

We will delete your account and all associated data from our systems
within 30 days of receiving your request.

## What gets deleted

- Your profile (name, date of birth, gender, country, contact details)
- All medications, doses, and dose history
- All vital sign readings, symptoms, and side effects
- Water, meal, exercise, and weight logs
- Emergency contacts
- Your profile photo, if you added one
- Your account's sign-in record with us

Nothing is retained after deletion, with one exception: if you ever used
**Contact us** inside the app, that specific message (sent to us by email
at the time) follows our normal email retention, separately from your
in-app account — deleting your account does not retroactively delete a
message you already sent us.
